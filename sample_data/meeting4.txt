Project Nimbus – Requirements Brainstorm
Date: 25 February 2025
Attendees: Rahul Sharma, Ananya Iyer, Vikram Rao, Sneha Kulkarni

Rahul: We're deciding whether to actually build Project Nimbus. Let's keep it focused.
The pitch is a campus event booking app where students can reserve seats for college
events and pay a small fee.

Sneha: I looked at three existing apps students use. All of them have terrible UX.
Two don't even let you filter by date. This is a real gap.

Ananya: I can sketch the core screens this week. Let's say three: event list, event
detail, and booking confirmation.

Vikram: What about the backend? We have no infrastructure.

Rahul: Decision — we'll use cloud-hosted services. No on-premise. That keeps it
simple and cost-effective.

Sneha: Do we need payment at all in the MVP? Could be free events only.

Rahul: That's a good question. Let's leave the payment scope open for now —
we'll decide after we talk to the Dean's office.

Vikram: What's our target launch?

Rahul: Rough goal — end of April. I'll come back with a formal timeline.

Ananya: I'll prepare a small mock-up for the booking flow. Give me until 28 February.

Sneha: Should we write a one-page project charter?

Rahul: Yes. Sneha, can you draft it by 1 March? Medium priority.

Vikram: One concern — if we choose the wrong tech stack now, we'll pay for it later.

Rahul: Agreed. We'll lock the stack in the kickoff meeting.

Sneha: Also, who owns the domain name and hosting account?

Rahul: Unresolved for now. We'll sort it after the kickoff.

Ananya: And what's our budget ceiling? That's also open.

Rahul: Correct. Both are open items for the kickoff.
